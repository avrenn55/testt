Good to know

Frequently asked questions

How long does delivery take?
Estimated delivery is 3–7 business days after an order request is reviewed and confirmed. This is a portfolio demo, so no actual dispatch is arranged.
What is your shipping policy?
Shipping is shown as complimentary on orders above ₹1,999. For this demo, shipping is calculated at ₹99 below that threshold. The shop owner would confirm final delivery details directly.
Can I return an item?
Eligible products are shown with a 7-day return window. Return eligibility and instructions would be confirmed by the shop owner before a real launch.
How do I place an order?
Choose a product, select any required size or colour, add it to your bag, and submit the order request form. This website does not take online payments.
How will my order be confirmed?
If the owner configures the Formspree endpoint, your request is sent to the configured shop inbox. The shop owner would contact you to confirm product availability, delivery and payment details. A successful form submission is not final order acceptance.
Can I change or cancel my order?
Contact the shop owner as soon as possible using the configured contact details. Changes and cancellations are not automated by this demo.
